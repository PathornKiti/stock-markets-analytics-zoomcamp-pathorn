HYPOTHESES
H1 (diagnostics): some of the 8 signals' IC is largely style exposure (size, beta, momentum, volatility,
   liquidity, sector); flagged if the Fama-MacBeth slope with style controls has |t| < 2 while the raw slope has t >= 2,
   or the mean cross-sectional R^2 of the signal on the styles (+ sector dummies) is > 0.5.
H2 (correlation control): a combination that accounts for signal correlation (ICIR-shrunk, Lowdin-orthogonalised,
   mean-variance on the IC covariance, cluster-equal) has a better walk-forward cost-adjusted top-decile spread than
   equal-weight or plain IC-weighted ranks.
H3 (construction): a cost-aware construction (alpha-hysteresis top-N, or constrained mean-variance with a linear cost
   term) applied to the walk-forward composite beats the S5 rule net of 0.168%/side, walk-forward 2016-26.

STAGE 1 - diagnostics (descriptive only; nothing here is used for selection): per signal, daily rank IC vs h = 5/10/20
alpha, ICIR, Newey-West t (lags = h); IC decay incl. marginal windows days 6-10 and 11-20 (differences of the
cumulative alphas); IC by year and by walk-forward HMM regime; decile monotonicity (D10-D1, Spearman of decile means);
rank autocorrelation (lags 1/5/20) and top-decile turnover; breakeven cost per side = top-decile spread / (2 x
turnover), weekly and monthly non-overlapping; Fama-MacBeth slopes (z-scored, daily) raw / with styles (size = log
60d traded value, beta 120d, momentum 12-1, volatility 60d, illiquidity = log Amihud 20d; a signal's own
characteristic is dropped from its controls) / with styles + sector; style exposures (mean daily Spearman with each
style; mean R^2 on styles + sector dummies).

STAGE 2 - combination schemes. Signal weights for year Y use only daily ICs (h = 5) whose forward window ends before
Y, expanding window from 2013 (>= 120 days, else equal weights; 2013 = equal weights):
  K1 EW8   equal-weight centred ranks of all 8                    K2 ICW   w ~ max(mean IC, 0)
  K3 ICIRS w = 0.5 x max(ICIR,0)/sum + 0.5 x 1/8 (shrink to equal) K4 ORTH  Lowdin (symmetric) orthogonalisation of
           the 8 z-scored rank vectors each day, then K2 weights from the orthogonalised signals' own past ICs
  K5 MVIC  w = argmax w'mu - 1/2 w'S w, w >= 0 (normalised to sum 1), mu = mean daily IC, S = Ledoit-Wolf
           covariance of the daily IC series                     K6 CLUS  average-linkage clustering of the signals on
           1 - corr(daily IC); cut at distance 0.5; mean rank within a cluster; equal weight across clusters with
           positive past mean IC
  plus the existing C1 (ML) and C2 (ML+V8 = S5's score).
WFC (walk-forward composite): for each year Y >= 2014 pick, among {C1, C2, K1..K6}, the composite with the highest
t-stat of its weekly NET top-decile spread over 2013..Y-1 (decision days every 5 trading days counted from each year's
first day, forward windows ending before Y): spread = mean 5d alpha of the top decile - universe mean, minus
2 x 0.168% x top-decile turnover since the previous decision day. 2013 uses K1. Reported: ENB (exp entropy of IC
correlation eigenvalues and (sum l)^2 / sum l^2), diversification ratio sum|w| sd / sqrt(w'Sw) on the IC covariance,
time-series correlation of daily ICs and of daily long-short (top - bottom tercile) returns, mean cross-sectional
score correlation.

STAGE 3 - construction grid (24 configs) = score {C2, WFC} x 12 constructions; all weekly (decision every 5 trading
days from each year's first day), long-only, fully invested in stocks, tradable top-100, decision at close, fill next
open, 0.168%/side, a held name is resized only if its weight moves >= 1% of equity (integrated_v1 rule):
  EW-B  N {10, 20}: top-N equal weight, keep a held name while in the top 3N (N10 on C2 = the S5 rule)
  TILT-B N {10, 20}: as EW-B, weights linear 1.5 -> 0.5 by score rank
  IV-B  N {10, 20}: as EW-B, weights ~ 1 / vol_60
  HYS   N {10, 20}: equal weight; cost-aware hysteresis - replace a held name by an outside candidate only if
        alpha_cand - alpha_held > 2 x 0.168% (round trip); alpha = Grinold IC_20 x idio_vol x sqrt(20) x z(score),
        IC_20 = the score's mean daily IC at h = 20 over data ending before Y (2013: 0.05 a priori)
  MVO   lambda {3, 30} x neutral {off, on}: max a'w - lambda/2 w'S w - 0.168% x sum|w - w0|; a as HYS (20-day),
        S = 20 x Ledoit-Wolf covariance of 120 daily returns; s.t. sum w = 1, 0 <= w <= 10%, sector <= 30%;
        candidates = top 40 by score + held (+ top 20 by traded value when neutral); neutral = |size z exposure -
        target| <= 0.5 and |beta z exposure - target| <= 0.5, target = traded-value-weighted tradable universe
        (SET100-like, closer to TDEX); if SLSQP fails the bands are dropped that day (count reported); weights < 0.5% -> 0.
