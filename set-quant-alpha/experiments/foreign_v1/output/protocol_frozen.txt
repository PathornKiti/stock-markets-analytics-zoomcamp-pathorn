BACKGROUND: Thai stocks have a foreign ownership limit (FOL). When foreign holdings reach it, foreigners can buy only
from other foreigners on the foreign board (<SYM>_f.BK), usually at a premium to the local board (Bailey & Jagtiani
1994, JFE; Bailey, Chung & Kang 1999). The premium and foreign-board activity measure foreign demand that the
local price has not absorbed. This is new information: no earlier experiment used it (all prior foreign-flow
features were market-level baht / EEM proxies).

DATA: LSEG daily foreign-board history (TRDPRC_1, ELG_ACVOL, ELG_TNOV, VWAP) for live RICs ever in SET100
(scripts/fetch_lseg.py foreign), local close from LSEG (same split adjustment), local traded value from the
production dataset (dvol). Targets, universe, ML/V8 scores, styles: integrated_v1 panel (tradable top-100,
beta-adjusted forward alpha, next open -> close t+h). Delisted names have no foreign-board RIC (not covered).

SIGNALS (defined a priori; a stock-day is covered only if the stock is tradable and the signal is defined)
  prem     foreign premium = f_close / local close - 1 on days with foreign-board volume > 0, carried forward at
           most 20 trading days. Prior: none (two-sided) - a high premium is foreign demand (+) but may mean-revert (-).
  dprem20  prem(t) - prem(t-20). Prior: + (rising foreign demand spills over to the local line).
  fshare20 20-day foreign-board traded value / 20-day local traded value. Prior: + (foreign-board activity =
           foreign buying pressure at the limit).

TESTS (cross-sectional, covered names only, days with >= 10 covered names)
  T1 daily Spearman IC vs 5-day and 20-day forward alpha; Newey-West t (lags = horizon). 3 signals x 2 horizons.
  T2 marginal value: daily Fama-MacBeth of the forward-alpha rank on ranks of [signal, C2 (S5 score),
     log size, beta, 12-1 momentum]; NW t of the signal slope.
  Also reported, not gated: coverage by year, IC by year, quintile spreads, IC by HMM regime.

PERIODS: discovery 2013-2021 (all decisions); holdout 2022-01 -> 2026-10 computed ONCE after the discovery decision
is written to output/discovery_decision.txt, and reported whatever it shows.

GATES (per signal-horizon)
  G1 discovery IC t >= 2 in the prior direction (prem: |t| >= 2, its sign becomes the prior for the holdout)
  G2 discovery marginal FM slope t >= 2, same sign
  G3 holdout IC same sign with t >= 1.5
  Pass G1-G3 -> candidate for a new paper shadow (C2 + signal blend), to be registered separately by the user.
  Nothing is deployed from this experiment.
TRIALS: 6 (3 signals x 2 horizons).
